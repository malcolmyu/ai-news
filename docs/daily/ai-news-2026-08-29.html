Aaron Levie @levie
This week’s tech earnings calls were a critical reminder of how valuable the relationship is between software and AI. Software provides the guardrails for how data is managed, how business logic in workflows are maintained, how you manage and govern access to information, how you protect data, and more. You want these systems to work the same way every time, without fail. Agents, on the other hand, work inside these systems and with this data to execute on tasks just like people would have. But at a scale far greater than people ever did, which is especially why the deterministic controls matter more than ever. And in fact many of the best ways to deploy agents will be directly within the software systems themselves, like from Salesforce, Box, Harvey, ServiceNow, and so on. These platforms can optimize agents for their workflows and domains, with evals and context that are unique to their vertical, as well as connect to other agents headlessly. The net result is both software and AI adoption go up, hand in hand. Software and Agents are going to work together, and ultimately grow the size of the IT TAM dramatically as a result over time.
本周的科技财报电话会再次提醒我们，软件与 AI 之间的关系有多宝贵。软件提供了数据如何管理、工作流中的业务逻辑如何维护、信息访问如何治理、数据如何保护等护栏，你希望这些系统每次都万无一失地工作。而 agent 则在系统内、用这些数据执行任务，就像人一样——但规模远超人类所能，这正是确定性控制比以往任何时候都更重要的原因。事实上，部署 agent 的许多最佳方式会直接落在软件系统内部，比如 Salesforce、Box、Harvey、ServiceNow 等，它们能针对自己的垂直领域优化 agent 的工作流与上下文。最终结果是软件和 AI 的采用率携手上升，并把 IT 总可寻址市场大幅做大。
