The AI jobs prediction so far is playing out the opposite of what many thought. The reason this is happening is because AI is bringing automation to areas that don’t have finite demand characteristics. AI allows companies to do far more than they could before, but agents still require human operators and oversight to generate value from them. And not only has AI not replaced many of the types of jobs that theoretically would have been most affected by AI - engineers or lawyers - but AI is also creating or growing entire categories of jobs across the economy. Cybersecurity, FDEs, agent operators, engineers in non-software domains, and tons of job categories are going to explode.
关于 AI 就业的预测，到目前为止的走向与很多人的预期相反。 原因在于，AI 正在自动化那些需求没有上限的领域。AI 让公司能做比以前多得多的东西，但智能体仍需人工操作和监管才能产生价值。 AI 没有取代理论上最该受影响的工程师、律师等岗位，反而在整个经济中创造或扩大了全新的岗位类别。 网络安全、FDE、智能体运维、非软件领域的工程师，以及大量岗位类别都将爆发。
