Builder 动态

Andrej Karpathy — AI 的 I/O 范式演进

前 Tesla AI 总监、OpenAI 创始成员发表了一篇长推，讨论 AI 与人之间输入输出交互范式的演进路径。核心观点：audio dominates IO now across almost all modalities — but human listening speed is the bottleneck. Voice is the preferred input mode for humans, but effective output must engage our visual system. 从 text-only → text+HTML → 交互式神经视频，每一次范式升级都重新定义了人机交互。
